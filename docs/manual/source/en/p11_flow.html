Chapter 11One sale, end to end

To see how all the layers fit together, let's follow a real case: Dad sells Rosa 2 cartons of 15 at ₡2,500 and 1 of 30 at ₡4,800, on credit, and Rosa pays ₡2,000 on the spot. Total: ₡9,800.

Step by step, with the files

  1. Ventas → "Nueva venta". SalesScreen navigates to /ventas/nueva?fecha=…. NewSaleScreen reads the period from the address (readView) and opens SaleForm with that day as the starting date. SaleForm requests the last prices (findLastPrices).
  2. Filling in the form. Choose "Fiado" in the "¿Cómo pagó?" Dropdown. Add two lines (SaleLines); each line is an OpenLine (saleLine.ts) and its subtotal shows instantly. Type ₡2,000 in "¿Pagó algo ahora?" (MoneyInput). Search for and choose Rosa in CustomerChooser.
  3. "Guardar venta". SaleForm checks what it can (each line's findProblems, a customer for the credit sale, the amount paid less than the total). If anything is wrong, it puts the error next to the field and moves focus there. If all is well, it opens SaleConfirmDialog: "Mixto: pagó ₡2.000 ahora y quedan ₡7.800 fiados" (mixed: paid ₡2,000 now, ₡7,800 on credit).
  4. "Listo". Inside useOneAtATime, it calls createSale(input) from services/sales.ts, which sends POST /api/sales with the JSON.
  5. On the server. SaleEndpoints receives the SaleCommand; SaleValidator checks it again and turns it into SaleFields. CreateSale.HandleAsync checks Rosa exists, creates the Sale, its two SaleItems with LineTotal (₡5,000 and ₡4,800) and the ₡9,800 total, and a ₡2,000 Payment with SaleId = this sale.
  6. Saved. SaveChangesAsync: AuditInterceptor sets CreatedAt/UpdatedAt; SQLite writes the 4 rows in one transaction, with synchronous = FULL.
  7. Response. SaleCredit.DescribeAsync builds the SaleDetail and works out with SaleStatuses what it owes now (₡7,800, if Rosa had no unused standalone payments). It answers 201 Created.
  8. Back in the interface. SaleForm marks that there are no unsaved changes (allowLeaving) and navigates straight to viewAfterSave(…): the same day (or week/month, if the date fits), on the Fiado tab. SalesScreen loads sales, payments and summary again; the "Entró hoy" card goes up ₡2,000 (the payment) and "Fiado hoy" goes up ₡9,800.
What happens if something goes wrong
  • If the server answers 400 (for example, Rosa was deleted just before), the error stays inside the confirmation window; "Editar" returns to the form with focus on the rejected field.
  • If the API doesn't answer within 10 seconds, the error stays in the same window with a message saying what to do. Nothing was half-saved, thanks to the transaction.
